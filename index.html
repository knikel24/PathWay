Our mission

Help every high school student make an informed career decision by understanding their strengths and getting real-world experience first.

Most students choose a college major or first job based on a guess. Pathway replaces the guess with two things: a clear picture of what they're good at, and a chance to see a career up close at a business in their own community.

  • Know your strengths, interests, and skills
  • Get matched with local businesses
  • Gain hands-on experience before you commit

How it works

Four steps from "I'm not sure" to "I've tried it."

  1. 1

    Discover Yourself

    Complete a short career and personality assessment that identifies your strengths, interests, and skills.

  2. 2

    Find Your Match

    Pathway analyzes your results and interests to suggest career fields and local businesses that may be a good fit.

  3. 3

    Gain Experience

    Join an internship, job-shadowing day, or short-term work experience with a participating business.

  4. 4

    Discover Your Future

    Use what you learned to make better decisions about college, training, or employment.

The assessment

How the assessment works

You answer 10 everyday scenarios, like how you'd handle a group project or what you'd do with a free period. Each answer adds points to eight strength areas. There are no wrong answers.

The assessment is inspired by career-interest tools such as the ASVAB Career Exploration Program, but it is not the ASVAB, and Pathway is not affiliated with the U.S. military or Department of Defense. This prototype is not scientifically validated.

    Matching

    How you're matched with opportunities

    1. We score your strengths

    Your answers become a profile across eight areas, from analytical thinking to working with your hands.

    2. We suggest career areas

    Each career area relies on certain strengths. We rank the fields that line up with your top results.

    3. We find local businesses

    Partner businesses list opportunities by career area. You see the ones near you that match your top fields.

    What you gain

    More than a line on your resume

    Short experiences help you rule careers in or out before you spend time and money on them.

    Ways to host

    Choose the experience that fits your team

    1–3 days

    Job shadowing

    A student follows an employee through a normal workday and asks questions. Lowest time commitment.

    4–8 weeks

    Internship

    A student or small team works on a defined project, like a marketing plan or process review, and presents results.

    Home / About Us

    We're closing the gap between school and career.

    Pathway is a student-led business concept built for an Entrepreneurship class.

    The problem

    Students choose careers without seeing them

    High school students are asked to pick a college major, a training program, or a job with very little real exposure to the work. Many choose based on what they've heard, then change direction later, which costs time and tuition. Local businesses, meanwhile, struggle to connect with young people who might become future employees.

    Mission

    Informed decisions through real experience

    Our mission is to help high school students make more informed career decisions by helping them understand their strengths and giving them opportunities to gain real-world experience.

    Vision

    Every student tries a career before choosing one

    We picture a future where every high school graduate has completed at least one hands-on experience in a field they're considering, through businesses in their own community.

    Why early exploration matters

    The earlier, the cheaper the lesson

    Learning that a career isn't a fit during a two-day job shadow costs almost nothing. Learning it after two years of college costs much more. Early exploration also helps students pick classes with purpose and gives them a stronger story for applications.
